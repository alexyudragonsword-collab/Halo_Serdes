The white clock's CDR error is the loop's own hunting plus the white edge jitter it
cannot follow; there is no model column for it: the statistical engine builds no CDR for
kind=white and takes the transmitter's RJ edge by edge, as noise through the channel,
not as a sampling error. For the PLL clocks the model column is the linearised loop's
sampling-instant sigma, 0.81-1.07x the measured. Its Mueller-Muller detector reads the
slicer input after the DFE's feedback, as the kernel does, and a loop this slow sees the
detector noise's low-frequency density -- on PRBS13Q the receiver noise's alone, the
pattern's share sitting on lines from 13.7 MHz up (ROADMAP P2 #24: before, up to 1.39x
over). The statistical BER is listed for the record: 0.4-0.5x of the time engine's at
kp_shift 8 and 1.2-2.2x at 9, a steep bathtub smeared by a sigma 0.87-0.89x and
1.05-1.07x the measured. On the white clock it is 4.5e-6 against the time engine's
4-5 errors here and 8.2e-6 over six seeds (ROADMAP P2 #25: as a sampling-instant
smear the jitter read 2.4e-7, a thirtieth). The comparison that is in scope is the CDR
