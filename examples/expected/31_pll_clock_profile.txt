The white clock's CDR error is the loop's own hunting plus the white edge jitter it
cannot follow; there is no model column for it: the statistical engine builds no CDR for
kind=white and takes the transmitter's RJ edge by edge, as noise through the channel,
not as a sampling error. For the PLL clocks the model column is the linearised loop's
sampling-instant sigma over a run this long, the loop starting from rest, and 'steady'
the same loop once it has rung up; the LMS FFE takes up part of the offset itself, a
second tracking path beside the loop (ROADMAP P2 #29). From kp_shift 7 the loop is
underdamped (its error response peaks near 1.1 MHz) and rings up far slower than 200k
symbols last, so a run holds 0.65-0.87x of its steady state. The measured column is one
seed, and what the loop leaves sits within a decade of its 0.7 MHz bandwidth, a few
cycles a run: one seed scatters 0.32-1.02 samples at kp_shift 9. This one reads
1.10-1.43x the model; the 16-seed RMS is 1.04-1.05x (until 2026-10-15 the model was the
